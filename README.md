# Hi, I'm Mathieu Grandjean

MSc Data Science student (research track) at **Centrale Lille / Université de Lille**, on a double degree with **IMT Nord Europe**, where I specialise in Data Science & AI.

I work on **detecting AI-generated images** and on **understanding what these detectors actually look at**.

> 🔎 **Looking for a research internship abroad** starting **13 April 2027** (8 to 14 weeks), in multimedia forensics, explainable AI or computer vision.

## Research interests

- **Multimedia forensics**: detection and source attribution of AI-generated images
- **Explainable AI**: attribution methods, concept-based explanations, and their limits
- **Vision-language models**: CLIP embeddings as forensic features

## Current work

- **Research project (2026 – 2027)**: explainability of CLIP-based synthetic image detectors.
  IMT Nord Europe, CERI Digital Systems, supervised by Vincent Itier and Juan Manuel Miramont.
- **Research internship (Apr. – Aug. 2026)**: built CLIP-based detectors (frozen encoder + lightweight classifier) for binary detection and multiclass source attribution.
  Ranked **31st / 92** in the DLMMDD Synthetic Image Attribution challenge (ICANN 2026), with **97.5% accuracy** and about 10 minutes of training.
  Applied LIME, Grad-CAM, Integrated Gradients, CRAFT and MACO, and assessed their limits in a forensic setting.

*The code from these projects is not public yet.*
